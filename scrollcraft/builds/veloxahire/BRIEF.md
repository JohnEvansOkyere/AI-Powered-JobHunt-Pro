# VeloxaHire redesign

Authored implementation decisions under the user's request: “this current looks too AI generated”; “use /nateherk-design:scroll-craft for the landing page”. Optional brand preferences requested during inspection.

## Eight brief topics

1. Vibe: professional, human, useful, composed. User references: LinkedIn and BambooHR.
2. Journey: discover work; explore a category; understand matching; prepare a CV; browse jobs.
3. Energy: calm opening, quiet directory, focused interactive peak, reassuring close.
4. Feeling curve: recognition through career photography; agency through category navigation; clarity through skill selection; confidence through editable CVs; readiness through a direct closing action.
5. Peak and signature: “I selected my skills and could see why a role belonged on my shortlist.” A transparent, explicitly illustrative matching example computes matched requirements and carries the role into real search.
6. Aesthetic: information-led white and soft grey surfaces, forest green, neutral sans-serif. No glows, invented statistics, testimonials, or feature-card grids.
7. Distinct scenes in normal flow. No scroll hijacking, cinematic pinning, or video.
8. Reuse the inspected logo and existing career photographs. No generation required; preflight has Chrome and ffmpeg, lacks playwright-core and an optional Kie key.

## Structure

Audience: candidates seeking local and remote work. Employers use the separate VeloxaRecruit destination. Belief: I can understand which roles fit and prepare an application I control. Primary action: Browse jobs. Account action: Create account.

Grammar: career desk. Working navigation and search open the page, followed by a directory, an operable matching example, a short CV explanation, and direct search close. The provided filmic, chaptered, live-surface, continuous-world, poster, gallery, split-stage and cutlist grammars each impose structures at odds with this practical introduction; this grammar forbids pinning, fake metrics and passive dashboard illustrations.

Layer contract: stable sage inset; photographic plane recedes gently; near caption travels independently. Type and controls stay stable outside the photograph. Mobile stacks search first and keeps the whole subject legible; reduced motion retains all content without travel.

| Beat | Feeling | Device |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Recognition | Layered parallax |
| Directory | Agency | Natural flow |
| Matching peak | Clarity | Interactive requirement matching and reveal |
| CV preparation | Confidence | Entrance |
| Close | Readiness | Static resolve |

Peak gets the largest content area. No authored blank scrolling. Tell-someone sentence: “It is the site where I can try my skills against a shortlist and understand why it changes.” First build has no prior fingerprints.

Research: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/ prioritizes categories and join/sign-in paths; https://www.bamboohr.com/ connects human outcomes with product explanation. Adapt hierarchy and tone without copying branding or unsupported claims.
